How to read notation

R L U D F B
Right, Left, Up, Down, Front, Back. Turn that face clockwise as you look straight at it.
R  R'  R2
No mark is a 90° clockwise turn. A prime ( ' ) is 90° counter-clockwise. A 2 is a 180° half turn.
r u l d f b
Lowercase (or a "w", like Rw) turns that face and the layer behind it together.
M E S
The three middle slices. M follows L, E follows D and S follows F.
x y z
Rotate the whole cube in the same direction as R, U and F.
( )
Brackets group a trigger such as (R U R' U'). Do it as one smooth chunk. They never change the moves.
